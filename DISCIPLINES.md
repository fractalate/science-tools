# Disciplines

Science Tools organizes formulas, constants, and calculators into seven disciplines. Shared items appear in each relevant field. Supporting constants accompany the formulas and calculators that use them.

## Subject scopes

| Discipline | Scope |
| --- | --- |
| Chemistry | Ideal gases, chemical bonding, electronegativity, ionic lattice energy, and J/eV conversion for bond energies. Coulomb’s law and electrostatic constants support ionic interactions. |
| Spectroscopy | Photon energy, reciprocal wavelength, hydrogen-like energy levels and transitions, Rydberg scales, and photon/molar energy conversion. |
| Classical mechanics | Nonrelativistic motion, momentum, springs, gravity, and circular kinematics. |
| Electromagnetism | Electrostatics and electromagnetic radiation, including photon energy and wavelength/frequency conversion. |
| Atomic physics | Isolated atoms and ions, Bohr approximations, spectral transitions, matter waves, Schrödinger evolution, and Coulomb interactions. |
| Trigonometry | Right-triangle ratios and general identities, including their domain restrictions and half-angle sign conventions. |
| Quantum physics | Schrödinger evolution, matter waves, photon energy, and associated constants and converters. |

The light calculator provides optional J/mol and eV/mol photon outputs. Avogadro’s constant appears wherever this calculator is available to support molar conversion. Elementary charge supports J/eV conversion. Bohr orbit formulas belong to Atomic physics and carry their model assumptions.

SI prefixes are shared reference material. Chemistry is the initial selection. Multiple selections display the union of their content without duplicate rows. With no selection, reference content is hidden. Filtering preserves input values and unit choices.

## References

Scientific references for the subject scopes:

- [NPTEL, Chemical Bonding: Lattice Energy](https://archive.nptel.ac.in/content/storage2/courses/104103069/module2/lec3/3.html): Born–Landé describes ionic crystal lattice binding.
- [IUPAC, electronegativity](https://goldbook.iupac.org/terms/view/E01990/plain): Pauling’s scale uses bond dissociation energies.
- [OpenStax Chemistry 2e, Strengths of Ionic and Covalent Bonds](https://openstax.org/books/chemistry-2e/pages/7-5-strengths-of-ionic-and-covalent-bonds): bonding and lattice energetics belong in chemistry.
- [OpenStax University Physics 3, The Hydrogen Atom](https://openstax.org/books/university-physics-volume-3/pages/8-1-the-hydrogen-atom): both Coulomb interaction and Schrödinger’s equation underpin atomic physics, with Bohr and modern quantum models distinguished.
- [OpenStax University Physics 3, Photons and Matter Waves](https://openstax.org/books/university-physics-volume-3/pages/6-summary): photon energy and de Broglie waves are foundational quantum topics.
- [OpenStax College Physics 2e, Photon Energies and the Electromagnetic Spectrum](https://openstax.org/books/college-physics-2e/pages/29-3-photon-energies-and-the-electromagnetic-spectrum): wavelength/frequency, photon energy, and J/eV conversion are relevant to electromagnetic radiation.
